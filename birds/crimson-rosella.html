Crimson Rosella

Quick Facts

  • Scientific name: Platycercus elegans
  • Family: Psittaculidae
  • Size: 32-36 cm
  • Weight: About 90-170 g
  • Wingspan: About 50-55 cm
  • Diet: Seeds, fruit, flowers, nectar and insects
  • Habitat: Forests, woodlands, riverine woodland, farmland and urban areas
  • Behaviour: Usually feeds in trees or on the ground and may occur singly, in pairs or in small groups
  • Activity: Diurnal
  • Nesting: A tree hollow containing decayed wood material
  • Breeding Season: September to January
  • Clutch Size: Usually 3-8 eggs
  • Call: Loud whistles and harsh chattering calls
  • Lifespan: Often lives for 10-15 years in the wild
  • Conservation Status: Least Concern
  • Where to Find: Australia: Queensland, New South Wales, Victoria, South Australia, Western Australia and Australian Capital Territory

About the Bird

The Crimson Rosella is a colourful eastern Australian parrot with brilliant crimson plumage, blue patches on the cheeks and wings, and a long blue tail in the typical form. Several geographical colour forms occur across its range. It feeds on seeds, fruit and flowers and can occur in forests as well as well-treed urban areas.
