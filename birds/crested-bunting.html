Crested Bunting

Quick Facts

  • Scientific name: Emberiza lathami
  • Family: Emberizidae
  • Size: 17-18 cm
  • Weight: About 20-30 g
  • Wingspan: About 25-29 cm
  • Diet: Seeds, grains and insects
  • Habitat: Grassland, scrub, agricultural fields and open woodland
  • Behaviour: Often feeds on the ground and perches prominently during the breeding season
  • Activity: Diurnal
  • Nesting: A cup-shaped nest placed on or close to the ground in vegetation
  • Breeding Season: April to July
  • Clutch Size: Usually 3-5 eggs
  • Call: Short metallic notes and a simple song
  • Lifespan: Often lives for several years in the wild
  • Conservation Status: Least Concern
  • Where to Find: India: northern and northeastern regions including Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh; Nepal; Bhutan; Bangladesh; Myanmar

About the Bird

The Crested Bunting is a distinctive Asian bunting with a prominent crest. Breeding males have rich chestnut and black plumage with contrasting markings, while females are duller. It is generally associated with open scrub, grassland, agricultural areas and woodland edges.
