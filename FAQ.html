FAQs

What is Aquila Birding?

Aquila Birding is a growing online birding project. We hope to one day document all the birds in the world.

If I spot an error in the bird-info pages, can I report it?

You can definitely report it, and we highly encourage it. Please help us improve our site so that everyone can enjoy a wonderful birding experience.

Where do the bird images come from?

We use images from Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay. An Acknowledgment page to acknowledge the photographers who took all those images is being made and will soon be ready.

Can I suggest a bird to be added?

You can absolutely suggest a bird. Use the email found in the Contact Us page to do so.

Will user accounts be added soon?

Yes, user accounts will soon be added. Users will be able to have a personalized experience with Aquila Birding.

How often is Aquila Birding updated?

Aquila Birding is updated very frequently. If not daily, weekly.
