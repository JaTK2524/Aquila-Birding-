About Us

Aquila Birding is a growing online birding project, founded on August 24th 2026, created to make learning about birds simple, interesting, and accessible to everyone. Our goal is to bring together information about different bird species, including their appearance, behaviour, habitat, calls, and other fascinating facts, all in one place. Whether you are an experienced birder, a student, or simply someone who enjoys watching the birds around you, we hope Aquila Birding helps you discover something new and develop a greater appreciation for the remarkable birds that share our world.

What You'll Find Here

  • Bird profiles
  • Scientific names and family information
  • Habitat and behaviour
  • Calls* and breeding information
  • More species being added regularly

Have a good time birding!
